01Introduction

Midnight Diary (the “App”) is published by LEON ALPHA LTD (“we”, “our”). Below we explain, in plain language, what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have.

02Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data when you use the App:

  • Device Information (e.g., device model, operating system, device identifiers)
  • Purchase information — if you buy an item in the App, Google Play sends the App a record of that purchase (order number, the item bought, the time of purchase and a purchase token). We never receive your payment card, bank or billing details.
  • Install attribution data — when you open the App, our attribution provider AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising ID, basic device and app information (model, OS version, App version, language), your IP address (used to derive your country) and the Google Play install referrer. See the Install Attribution section.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Unlock and restore the upgrade you buy, and prevent fraudulent or duplicate purchases
  • Measure which advertising campaigns brought new users to the App, and detect fraudulent installs

04Data Stored on Your Device

Your journal entries, the prompts you answer and the App's settings (including the optional lock) are saved on your device. Your entries are private to you: we never receive, read or analyse them.

This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include it in your own Google backup, which we cannot access.

05Ads

There are no ads in Midnight Diary. We do not share your data with advertising networks and it is not used for ad targeting. We do, however, use an install attribution provider to measure which advertising campaigns bring new users to the App — see the Install Attribution section.

06In-App Purchases

Midnight Diary is free to download and offers an optional one-time in-app purchase («Pro») that unlocks extra features. All payments are processed by Google Play (Google LLC): your payment details are handled by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy, and are never shared with us.

  • What we receive: only the purchase record described in the Information We Collect section, which the App uses to unlock Pro and to restore it after a reinstall or on another device signed in to the same Google account. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it is tied to your Google account and Google Play restores it automatically.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • Parents: every purchase needs the approval of the Google account holder. You can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play app (Settings → Authentication, or Google Family Link).

07Install Attribution

Midnight Diary uses AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) to measure how users found the App — for example, which advertising campaign led to an install — and to detect fraudulent installs. AppsFlyer processes the data described in the Information We Collect section on our behalf and only on our instructions. We record installs and app sessions only: no in-app activity events, no purchase details and no contact information are sent. The App sends this data when it is opened. You can limit it at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can ask us to delete the data held about your device (see the How to Reach Us section). AppsFlyer handles this data under the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

08Third-Party Services

Some features rely on service providers, and we may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data.

09Data Retention and Deletion

Data collected by the App is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above and is deleted automatically within 90 days. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time — write to us at the address in the How to Reach Us section and we will act on your request promptly. What you keep in the App yourself (see the Data Stored on Your Device section) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Purchase records the App receives are kept on your device only for as long as the App is installed; Google keeps its own payment records under Google's policies (see the In-App Purchases section).

10Device Permissions

The App asks only for what its features need:

  • Notifications — to show the optional reminders you set up. They are scheduled on your device, and you can turn them off in the App or in your Android settings.
  • Internet access — to process purchases through Google Play and measure installs with AppsFlyer. The App's own features work without an account.

11How We Protect Data

Information the App sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and we limit access to it to what the purposes in this policy require. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

12Children's Privacy

The App is meant for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them; if you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in the How to Reach Us section.

14Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
